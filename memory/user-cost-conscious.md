The user (Sureboy Realty project) is cost-conscious about token usage and asks for an effort/token estimate before committing to larger features.

**Why:** They asked "how many tokens will it consume?" before approving the signed-URL video protection work, and earlier asked whether a custom player would "take much token."

**How to apply:** For non-trivial features, proactively give a rough token/effort estimate and offer a cheaper phased option (e.g. do the cheap layer now, the expensive layer later) before diving in. They responded well to phased recommendations.
